# Try Website Accessibility Walkthrough and share what would make it more useful

This free browser tool is for nottingham business owners. Try six everyday tasks on your own website, then leave with a clear list of things to improve.

[Open the tool](https://web-design-nottingham.github.io/keyboard-drawer-lab/). No coding knowledge, installation or account is needed. Start with the fictional example, or fill in the labelled fields with your own information. A practical list to share with your website designer.

Feedback about unclear questions, missing practical options or mobile usability is welcome here. Please use fictional information in public examples and keep customer or confidential details out of this discussion.

These are introductory checks. They do not establish accessibility compliance or replace a full accessibility review.

Maintained by [Nottingham Web Design](https://webdesignnottingham.uk/), operated by JW Digital Services Ltd. The original developer helpers are still available in the repository, but they are not needed to use the browser tool.
